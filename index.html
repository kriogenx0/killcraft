KILLCRAFT

Mine ore, build your base from the sidebar, and destroy everything the enemy owns.

Build: click a structure in the sidebar. When it says READY, click it again and place it near your base. Right-click to hold or cancel.
Mouse: left-click or drag to select. Right-click to move, attack, harvest ore or set a rally point.
Touch: tap to select or give an order. Drag to scroll.
WASD scroll · Q/E or middle-drag turn · wheel zoom · Shift+wheel tilt · F attack-move · X stop · Ctrl+1-9 group · H base · Space last alert · P pause
