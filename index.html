KILLCRAFT

Mine ore, build your base from the sidebar, and destroy everything the enemy owns.

Build: click a structure in the sidebar. When it says READY, click it again and place it near your base. Right-click to hold or cancel.
Mouse: left-click or drag to select. Right-click to move, attack, harvest ore or set a rally point.
Touch: tap to select or give an order. Drag to scroll, two fingers to pinch, zoom and turn. Press and hold, then drag to box-select several units; hold on a unit to select all of its kind. Or turn on Select in the bottom bar to drag boxes and tap to add units; Army selects all your fighters.
WASD scroll · Q/E or middle-drag turn · scroll pan · Ctrl+wheel or pinch zoom · Alt+wheel tilt · M move · X stop · G/F/V stance · Ctrl+1-9 group · H select base · R repair · Del scrap · +/- zoom · [/] speed · Space last alert · P pause
Hotkeys: select a building and its sidebar items light up with a letter. Press it to build, train or research. Walls, defenses and Aether structures take two keys: H L T lays a Stone Wall.
